01 · The problem

The match moves.
The social desk searches.

A name is called

The announcer names a player. Someone starts hunting for his clips.

A pattern forms

A 4 v 2 builds in the box. Nobody tags it while it happens.

The moment goes cold

By the time the post is approved, the conversation has moved on.

Who has it: social and content teams at clubs, leagues and broadcasters

One minute. The people with this problem are the social and content teams at clubs and broadcasters. During a live match they are three steps behind: they hear a name, they search for clips, they approve a post. We wanted to collapse those steps into one screen.
