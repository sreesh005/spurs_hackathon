Three minutes across this slide and the next two. Twelve Labs does three jobs. Marengo 3.0 indexes the match library on visual and audio. Pegasus 1.5 describes clips. The asset transcription endpoint gives timed sentences that we scan for player names; each sentence reaches the screen when the video reaches it, so the words and the picture stay together.
